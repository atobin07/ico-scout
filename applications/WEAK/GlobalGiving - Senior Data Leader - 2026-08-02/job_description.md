# Job Description: Senior Data Leader (Individual Contributor)
**Company:** GlobalGiving (nonprofit — donor/nonprofit marketplace)
**Location:** Not specified (remote-friendly)
**Type:** Full-time, Individual Contributor reporting to senior leadership
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-08-02

## Overview
High-impact IC data leader to unlock the full potential of GlobalGiving's rich donor marketplace and nonprofit partner data. Own the full data stack — from raw ETL through data modeling, analytics engineering, executive dashboards, and governance — with opportunity to build a team over time.

## Primary Responsibilities
- **Executive Partnership & Discovery**: Conduct structured discovery interviews with executives and functional leaders; translate business questions into prioritized data roadmaps; serve as translator between business strategy and data architecture
- **Data Modeling & Architecture**: Audit existing Salesforce CRM and cloud data warehouse data model; design revised model for multi-sided marketplace (donors and nonprofits); own and evolve dbt project
- **Data Engineering & Operations**: Manage ETL pipelines (Salesforce, payment processors, web analytics); define and enforce data quality standards; evaluate tooling
- **Analytics & Reporting**: Build executive dashboards and self-serve reporting; surface donor behavior and partner performance insights; establish data literacy culture
- **Data Governance**: Shared KPI definitions, source-of-truth standards, metadata documentation, stewardship

## Required Qualifications
- 10-15+ years in data engineering, analytics engineering, or data science, with 3+ years in senior/lead capacity
- Proven one-person or founding data team experience — full stack from raw data to executive dashboard
- Deep hands-on dbt and modern cloud data warehouse (Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift)
- Strong SQL and Python
- Stakeholder discovery and technical roadmap translation
- Data model design/refactoring in production
- Executive and Board-level communication

## Preferred Qualifications
- Marketplace platform experience
- Donor management, fundraising metrics, nonprofit financial data
- B2B data product experience
- Looker, Metabase, Mode, or Tableau
- MySQL, Redshift, GitHub
- Salesforce data integration and CRM object model familiarity
- AI tools (Claude Code, GitHub Copilot)
- Mission-driven organization experience
- Experience hiring and growing a data team
