## Overview
Consumer products company seeking an interim Financial Analyst to support FP&A, Demand Planning, Supply Chain, and operational finance during a growth period. Key focus on AI-enabled forecasting tools to automate manual processes and improve decision-making.

## Key Responsibilities
- Support FP&A, budgeting, forecasting, and demand planning initiatives
- Develop, maintain, and enhance detailed bottom-up financial models and forecasts
- Validate forecasting assumptions and continuously improve forecast accuracy
- Partner with Operations, Planning, Procurement, Manufacturing, and Finance
- Analyze inventory, demand trends, and operational metrics
- Identify supply chain efficiencies and cost savings through financial analysis
- Standardize reporting processes across manufacturing and supply chain partners
- Leverage AI tools (including Claude) to automate forecasting and reporting processes
- Drive continuous process improvements for scalability

## Required Qualifications
- 3–5 years in FP&A, Financial Analysis, or related finance function
- Experience supporting both FP&A and Demand Planning
- Background in consumer products, manufacturing, food & beverage, retail, or inventory-intensive industries required
- Strong financial modeling, forecasting, and analytical skills
- Advanced Excel proficiency (complex financial models)
- Experience utilizing AI tools to improve finance processes (highly preferred)
