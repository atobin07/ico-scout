# Job Description: Senior Data Analyst
**Company:** Scaled Health
**Location:** Remote (US Citizen required)
**Type:** Full-time
**Pay:** Not specified
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-07-11

## Overview
Healthcare technology company focused on data connectivity and system security. Senior Data Analyst role driving data strategies using Epic's Cogito suite — Clarity, Caboodle, Reporting Workbench, SlicerDicer, and Radar — to support clinical, operational, and financial stakeholders.

## Key Responsibilities
- Develop, optimize, and maintain reports, dashboards, and data extracts within Epic Cogito tools
- Build and tune SQL queries, views, and stored procedures for custom analytics
- Power BI reporting
- Maintain Clarity and Caboodle data models; participate in ETL workflows
- Collaborate with business users to translate needs into technical solutions
- Ensure data accuracy, integrity, and compliance with governance and privacy standards
- Support and train report consumers; document specifications and maintain version control
- Partner with analytics and data teams to align reporting with enterprise goals

## Required Qualifications
- US Citizenship required
- **Epic Clarity certification required** (additional Epic module certifications preferred)
- Bachelor's degree in CS, Statistics, Economics, Math, or related; Master's preferred
- 5+ years in Business Intelligence, data analytics, or SQL development
- 3+ years with Epic Cogito tools (Clarity, Caboodle, or similar)
- Strong SQL, healthcare data/Epic workflow experience, Power BI or Tableau, communication skills
