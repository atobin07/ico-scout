## Summary
Fully remote operations coordinator supporting day-to-day business activities across internal teams. Coordinate documentation, maintain organized business records, and support administrative workflows for operations, finance, compliance, and client services.

## Key Responsibilities
- Coordinate and maintain corporate records, operational documentation, and internal business information
- Review documents for accuracy, consistency, and completeness before internal processing
- Prepare, organize, and retrieve information for reporting and administrative requests
- Maintain structured digital filing systems and documentation management practices
- Track documentation updates and maintain organized business records
- Handle confidential client and company information with discretion
- Follow policies for documentation management, information security, and regulatory compliance

## Requirements
- Experience supporting business operations, documentation, or administrative processes
- Ability to maintain organized information and identify inconsistencies
- Comfortable using business software to manage documentation and workflows
- Strong written communication and professional correspondence
- Sound judgment with confidential information
- Ability to adapt to changing priorities
