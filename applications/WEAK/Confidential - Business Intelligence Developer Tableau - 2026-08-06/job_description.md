# Job Description: Business Intelligence Developer (Tableau)
**Company:** Confidential
**Location:** Not specified
**Type:** Full-time
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-08-06

## Overview
Tableau Developer to lead BI development and server administration, transforming complex data into actionable strategic insights. Serves as internal consultant and data champion linking business users to technical infrastructure.

## Key Responsibilities
- Collaborate with internal users and external vendors to translate business objectives into data-driven action plans
- Use advanced SQL and data storage techniques to curate and unify data from multiple sources into cohesive data models
- Build, publish, and maintain interactive advanced dashboards for end-users
- Manage and administer Tableau Server environment (sites, groups, workbooks, database views, connections)
- Facilitate stakeholder meetings to identify efficiencies and drive process alignment
- Design, maintain, and enforce Tableau best practices, security, and governance policies
- Oversee application initiatives and development requirements from needs analysis to delivery

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, MIS, Business Administration, or related field (or equivalent)
- 4+ years of related IT experience including software development, program leadership, and business analysis
- Advanced proficiency in Tableau reporting, analytics, and dashboard content creation
- Strong SQL and relational database skills
- Proven ability to maintain, support, and upgrade a Tableau server environment
- Business needs analysis, process documentation, and complex data problem-solving
- Ability to translate technical concepts for non-technical stakeholders
- Exceptional written and oral communication skills

## Preferred Certifications
- Tableau Certification
- IIBA Business Analyst (CBAP)
