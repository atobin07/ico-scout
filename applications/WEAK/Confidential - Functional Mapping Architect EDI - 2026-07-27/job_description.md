# Job Description: Functional Mapping Architect (EDI & Integration)
**Company:** Confidential
**Location:** Not specified
**Type:** Contract (6–10 weeks)
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-07-27

## Overview
Functional Mapping Architect to assess and document enterprise EDI and integration platforms — mapping frameworks, trading partner onboarding, business rules, and end-to-end transaction processing. Partners with business stakeholders, architects, and engineering teams to document current-state capabilities, identify gaps and technical debt, and recommend modernization improvements.

## Key Responsibilities

### Current-State Assessment
- Assess enterprise EDI mapping frameworks, integration architecture, and functional capabilities
- Document end-to-end EDI transaction flows, mapping processes, and business workflows
- Analyze trading partner onboarding, configuration, and mapping lifecycle
- Identify dependencies across integration platforms, scheduling services, APIs, databases, and backend applications
- Review mapping standards, validation rules, routing logic, acknowledgements, and partner-specific processing

### Knowledge Acquisition & Documentation
- Conduct workshops and technical interviews with business and technical stakeholders
- Capture undocumented business processes, operational workflows, and system knowledge
- Develop architecture diagrams, workflow documentation, and integration process maps
- Build and maintain a comprehensive knowledge repository

### Architecture & Modernization Assessment
- Identify technical debt, architectural gaps, operational risks, and maintainability challenges
- Evaluate mapping governance, deployment processes, version management, and support models
- Recommend opportunities to simplify, standardize, and modernize integration and mapping capabilities
- Contribute to future-state architecture and modernization recommendations

### Collaboration
- Partner with Solution Architects, Technical Architects, Business Analysts, Product Owners, and Engineering teams
- Support knowledge transfer activities for implementation teams
- Present assessment findings and architecture documentation to stakeholders

## Required Qualifications
- 8–10+ years enterprise EDI, system integration, or mapping architecture experience
- Strong knowledge of ANSI X12, EDIFACT, XML, JSON, flat-file, and API-based integrations
- Experience with enterprise integration platforms: IBM Sterling, BizTalk, Boomi, MuleSoft, OpenText, Seeburger, Cleo, webMethods, or similar
- Deep understanding of trading partner onboarding, EDI transaction processing, mapping lifecycle, and integration architecture
- Experience analyzing and documenting complex legacy systems and business workflows
- Strong analytical, facilitation, stakeholder management, and technical documentation skills

## Preferred Qualifications
- Supply chain, retail, manufacturing, healthcare, logistics, or financial services integration experience
- Cloud integration platforms and API management familiarity
- Event-driven architecture and enterprise integration patterns
- Application modernization or digital transformation experience
