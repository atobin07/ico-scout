# Job Description: RFx Operations Contractor
**Company:** Data Skill Source
**Location:** Fully Remote (US)
**Type:** Contract
**Pay:** $30–$35 CAD/hour, 30+ hours/week
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-07-01

## Overview
Support proposal and RFP execution for an enterprise SaaS client. Manage and deliver high-quality responses to RFPs, RFIs, security questionnaires, and other strategic sales requests.

## Responsibilities

### RFx & Proposal Execution
- Support end-to-end delivery of RFx responses (RFPs, RFIs, security questionnaires, etc.)
- Manage proposal timelines, milestones, and internal deadlines to ensure on-time submission
- Track requirements, inputs, and open items across multiple stakeholders
- Ensure all responses meet customer requirements and submission guidelines

### Cross-Functional Coordination
- Work closely with Sales, Solutions Engineering, Product, Security, Legal, and Finance teams
- Consolidate inputs from subject-matter experts and resolve gaps or inconsistencies
- Coordinate internal reviews and approvals as needed (legal, pricing, security, leadership)

### Content & Quality Control
- Draft, edit, and refine proposal content (executive summaries, cover letters, standard responses)
- Improve clarity, consistency, and customer-focused messaging across proposal materials
- Ensure final deliverables meet quality standards for formatting, grammar, and alignment

### Process & Documentation
- Maintain proposal trackers, documentation, and artifacts in shared tools and repositories
- Follow established proposal governance and review processes
- Contribute to improving templates, workflows, and content libraries over time

## Required Qualifications
- 1–4 years of experience in proposal management, project management, sales operations, or related role
- Hands-on experience supporting RFPs, RFIs, or complex B2B customer requests
- Strong organizational skills with ability to manage multiple deadlines simultaneously
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- High attention to detail and strong sense of ownership

## Nice to Have
- Experience in SaaS, enterprise software, or technology companies
- Familiarity with proposal management tools, CRM systems, or shared content libraries
- Understanding of enterprise sales cycles or procurement processes
- Experience editing or structuring technical or solution-oriented content
