# Job Description: Monday.com Operations Specialist
**Company:** Americas' SAP Users' Group (ASUG)
**Location:** Remote
**Type:** Contract (15 flex hours/week through EOY 2026, possible extension)
**Compensation:** $55/hr
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-07-27

## Overview
Monday.com Operations Specialist to manage ASUG's Monday.com environment — building boards, maintaining automations, supporting integrations with HubSpot and Zapier, deploying AI features, and serving as internal go-to for the platform. Marketing department support role.

## Key Responsibilities
- Build, update, and manage boards to track marketing projects and team tasks
- Set up and maintain automation "recipes" to reduce manual work and ensure data consistency
- Maintain integrations between Monday.com, HubSpot, and Zapier
- Deploy and test Monday.com AI tools
- Audit boards and workspaces to keep them organized and user-friendly
- Primary internal support contact for boards and dashboards

## Qualifications
- Required: Current Monday.com certification (Work Management Core or Admin Associate)
- 2–5 years hands-on experience as Monday.com administrator or power user
- Strong understanding of automation builders, board design, and external integrations (Zapier, HubSpot)
- Detail-oriented, organized, problem-solving mindset
