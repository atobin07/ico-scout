## Overview
Fast-growing digital marketing agency seeking an experienced SEO Lead to lead strategy and execution across multiple accounts, mentor team members, and shape the SEO department.

## Responsibilities
- Lead the SEO team; ensure consistent performance, quality, and execution across all accounts
- Develop, refine, and standardize SEO strategies, processes, and best practices
- Oversee account performance; ensure KPIs are consistently met or exceeded
- Provide strategic direction for high-priority and complex SEO initiatives
- Mentor, train, and support SEO team members across all experience levels
- Conduct regular audits and reviews to maintain quality and performance standards
- Collaborate with Paid Ads, Web, and Operations teams
- Identify opportunities for process improvement, scalability, and efficiency
- Stay current on algorithm updates, industry trends, and changes in search behavior

## Requirements
- Deep expertise in technical SEO, on-page SEO, and local SEO/GBP optimization
- Strong leadership and team management skills
- Strategic thinker; ability to build scalable SEO frameworks
- Advanced analytical and problem-solving abilities
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills
- High attention to detail and commitment to quality

## Preferred
- Experience managing multiple SEO accounts or campaigns simultaneously
- Proficiency with SEMrush, Ahrefs, Google Analytics, Google Search Console
- Agency or fast-paced digital marketing experience
- Understanding of website UX, conversion optimization, and web performance
