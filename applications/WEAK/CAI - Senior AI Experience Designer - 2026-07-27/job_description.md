# Job Description: Senior AI Experience Designer
**Company:** CAI (Consultants to America's Industry)
**Req Number:** R8006
**Location:** Remote (Full-time)
**Type:** Full-time
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-07-27

## Overview
Senior AI Experience Designer to design and deliver human-centered AI interaction experiences including prompts, workflows, feedback loops, and AI-enabled tools. Operates at the intersection of UX design, prompt engineering, agentic workflow design, and business AI enablement.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design the interaction layer for AI systems: prompts, workflows, feedback loops, escalation paths, human override mechanisms
- Own end-user experience design for agentic AI systems, copilot tools, and AI-augmented workflows from concept through deployment
- Translate AI architecture into intuitive, trusted user experiences that drive adoption
- Collaborate with Applied AI Architects and cross-functional teams
- Facilitate workshops and working sessions with stakeholders to gather requirements, validate use cases, test interaction models
- Design onboarding experiences and adoption pathways for AI enablement
- Develop prompt frameworks and contribute to prompt engineering standards
- Create user-facing materials: playbooks, guides, workflow documentation
- Monitor adoption signals, gather feedback, and continuously improve AI experiences
- Produce design artifacts: wireframes, journey maps, workflow diagrams, interaction flows

## Required Qualifications
- 8+ years UX design, product design, service design, or related human-centered discipline
- Experience designing AI-powered or AI-augmented products and workflows
- Hands-on prompt engineering for LLMs in real-world environments
- Strong systems thinking — multi-step AI workflows and human-AI interaction models
- Experience driving adoption and behavior change
- Strong facilitation and stakeholder engagement skills
- Proficiency with Figma, Miro, or similar design platforms
- Clear and actionable design artifacts in fast-moving environments
- Experience designing accessible solutions aligned to WCAG standards
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience designing AI solutions within enterprise environments
- Familiarity with agentic AI systems and human-in-the-loop interaction models
- Experience with ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, or similar tools professionally
- Background in change management, organizational design, or learning experience design
- Familiarity with public sector/regulated environments (FedRAMP, NIST)
- Experience building scalable design systems, prompt libraries, or reusable interaction patterns
- Experience in consulting or professional services delivery
