# Job Description: Project Manager, Research and Data Workflow
**Company:** American Heart Association (Periodic Table of Food Initiative)
**Location:** Remote (Home-based)
**Type:** Full-time, grant-funded (1-year, may extend)
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-06-29

## Overview
The Project Manager, Research and Data Workflow serves as a central operational and scientific coordination role within The Periodic Table of Food Initiative (PTFI), supporting the full lifecycle of omics-enabled research from study design through data analysis and translation.

## Responsibilities
- Coordinate research studies and deliverables across the PTFI ecosystem, including timelines, milestone tracking, data/metadata handoffs, and reporting
- Facilitate research collaboration across laboratories, researchers, and technical teams
- Support study design and execution in partnership with PIs and laboratories
- Coordinate procurement, tracking, and chain-of-custody of food samples, biospecimens, and associated metadata
- Ensure datasets meet defined standards for analysis readiness and cross-omics integration
- Coordinate quality control processes across omics platforms (LC-MS/MS metabolomics, lipidomics, proteomics; ICP-MS ionomics)
- Review and track batch effects, drift, missingness, internal standards/QC samples, normalization alignment, outliers, and sample identity
- Implement documentation and provenance pipelines including metadata schemas, QC reports, processing logs, dataset versioning, and data dictionaries
- Support PIs and collaborators with data analysis workflows and interpretation
- Contribute to manuscript preparation, figures, and reproducible supplements
- Develop or coordinate interactive dashboards and standalone tools (e.g., R/Shiny)
- Collaborate with data scientists to prepare ML-ready datasets and support advanced analytics
- Contribute to development of AI-enabled tools, indices, and scoring systems

## Qualifications
- MSc or PhD strongly preferred (bioinformatics, computational biology, analytical chemistry, biomedical informatics, food sciences)
- 3+ years experience supporting multi-omics or high-dimensional datasets
- Experience with study design, sample logistics, and publication development
- Hands-on understanding of omics data QC (MS-based peak/feature tables, batch effects, normalization, etc.)
- Experience with reproducible data analysis workflows in R (R Markdown/Quarto, Shiny apps)
- Knowledge of data integrity, security, and compliance (IRB, HIPAA)
- Strong written communication skills
- Ability to travel up to 10%
