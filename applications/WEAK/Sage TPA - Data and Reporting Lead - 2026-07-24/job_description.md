# Job Description: Data and Reporting Lead
**Company:** Sage TPA
**Location:** Remote (U.S.-based; Texas residency preferred, Central time zone strongly preferred)
**Type:** Full-time
**Pay:** Not specified
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-07-24

## Overview
Individual contributor role owning how Sage TPA measures, reports on, and learns from data across a multi-system environment (Salesforce, Tableau, SharePoint, Excel, CSV vendor feeds). Sets reporting standards, builds and maintains report templates, trains staff, and pulls data for operational, employer-facing, and executive needs across Consulting, Operations, Network & Claims, Clinical Advocacy, Marketing, and Finance.

## Key Responsibilities
- Set and maintain reporting standards (look/feel, naming conventions, structure, content)
- Design, build, and maintain library of report templates (operational, employer-facing, executive, ad hoc)
- Pull and reconcile data across systems; document data definitions; maintain single source of truth
- Train staff; provide first-line technical support; develop self-service reporting capability
- Gather requirements from stakeholders and translate into reporting solutions
- Accompany executives to internal and client meetings to explain reports
- Maintain Sage data dictionary and KPI definitions
- Partner with VP of IT Operations on eventual data lake/warehouse design
- Build and maintain 835, 837, and custom CSV data feed templates for vendor sharing
- Document report logic, data sources, refresh frequency, and caveats

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Information Systems, Business Analytics, CS, Statistics, Finance, or related
- 4–7 years in data, reporting, BI, or analytics with increasing responsibility
- Healthcare, TPA, insurance, or benefits industry experience preferred
- Strong SQL skills
- Hands-on BI/visualization experience (Tableau preferred; Power BI acceptable)
- Salesforce reporting and dashboards experience
- Advanced Excel (pivot tables, complex formulas, Power Query or VBA helpful)
- Experience extracting data via API (Salesforce REST/SOAP, Postman, Python/Node)
- Data modeling fundamentals (star schema, fact/dimension, ETL)
- Familiarity with healthcare EDI formats (837, 835, eligibility files)
- Texas residency preferred; Central time zone strongly preferred
