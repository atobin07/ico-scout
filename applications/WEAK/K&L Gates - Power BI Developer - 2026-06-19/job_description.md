## Job Description

At K&L Gates, we are looking for smart, imaginative and hard-working people with diverse backgrounds, experiences and ideas to join us. Perhaps our search for talented visionaries and your search for important and impactful work lead to the same place.

We are seeking an experienced Power BI Developer with at least 5 years of experience designing, developing, and optimizing Power BI dashboards and semantic data models.
The ideal candidate will be proficient in SQL, DAX, performance tuning, and experienced in Microsoft Fabric, including Direct Lake mode, Medallion architecture, data mesh concepts, and semantic model implementation.

Hands-on experience with Microsoft Copilot in Power BI is highly desirable to assist in AI-powered dashboard development. The developer will work closely with business stakeholders, data engineers, and developers in an Agile environment to deliver high-quality, scalable, and governed data solutions.

**Key Responsibilities:**

Power BI & Semantic Model Development
- Design, build, and maintain interactive, high-performing Power BI dashboards.
- Implement semantic models in Microsoft Fabric to enable reusable datasets, governed calculations, and enterprise-level analytics.
- Use DAX to define complex measures, KPIs, and business logic.
- Leverage Microsoft Copilot to assist in report creation, analysis, and insight generation.
- Ensure semantic models and datasets follow best practices for scalability, performance, and governance.

Data Analysis & KPI Validation
- Validate KPIs and metrics against source systems using SQL and business rules.
- Analyze datasets for accuracy, completeness, and alignment with business requirements.
- Collaborate with business analysts and stakeholders to translate requirements into semantic models and visualizations.

Microsoft Fabric & Direct Lake
- Build and manage datasets using Direct Lake mode in Microsoft Fabric.
- Apply knowledge of Medallion architecture (Bronze, Silver, Gold layers) and data mesh concepts.
- Collaborate with data engineers to ensure semantic models integrate seamlessly with data pipelines.

Performance Optimization & Troubleshooting
- Diagnose and resolve Power BI performance issues, including DAX, model design, and dashboard responsiveness.
- Optimize SQL queries used in underlying data sources.

Collaboration & Agile Delivery
- Partner with business stakeholders, data engineers, and BI developers.
- Participate in Agile ceremonies (sprint planning, stand-ups, reviews, retrospectives).

**Required Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Analytics, or related field.
- 5+ years as a Power BI Developer or BI Engineer.
- Advanced Power BI development and semantic model implementation in Microsoft Fabric.
- Strong proficiency in SQL and DAX.
- Hands-on experience with Microsoft Copilot in Power BI.
- Experience with Direct Lake mode, Medallion architecture, and data mesh concepts.

**Technical Skills:**
- Power BI – report development, DAX, semantic modeling, performance tuning
- Microsoft Fabric – Direct Lake, semantic models, data pipeline integration
- Microsoft Copilot – AI-assisted dashboard development
- SQL – advanced querying, joins, aggregations, data validation
- Agile tools: Jira, Azure DevOps, Confluence

**Preferred:**
- Python or Power Query (M)
- Row-level security (RLS) and data access governance
- Microsoft Purview
- Power BI integration with Azure DevOps, SharePoint, or Teams
