# Job Description: AI Analyst (Remote)
**Company:** Confidential
**Location:** Remote — Local to NY/NJ preferred
**Type:** Full-time
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-08-06

## Overview
Seeking an AI Analyst to design, develop, and implement enterprise AI solutions that solve complex business challenges and improve operational efficiency. Requires end-to-end delivery from concept through production: solution design, model selection, development, testing, deployment, monitoring, and optimization.

## Required Skills
- Azure AI, Microsoft Copilot Studio, Gemini Enterprise, or similar enterprise AI platforms
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- AI Agents and agent orchestration frameworks
- Prompt engineering
- Python and AI/ML libraries
- SQL and data analysis
- Integrating AI solutions with APIs, databases, and enterprise applications
- Cloud-based AI services
- Git, DevOps, CI/CD, and version control
- AI/ML concepts, model evaluation, model limitations, and Responsible AI practices

## Key Responsibilities
- Partner with business and technology teams to identify AI-driven opportunities
- Design, prototype, develop, test, deploy, and support enterprise AI applications
- Evaluate and select appropriate AI models, frameworks, and architectures
- Develop prompts, AI workflows, intelligent agents, and RAG pipelines
- Integrate AI solutions with enterprise systems, APIs, databases, and business processes
- Monitor AI solution performance, reliability, adoption, and accuracy
- Ensure compliance with enterprise security, governance, privacy, and Responsible AI standards
- Create technical documentation, architecture diagrams, testing results, and implementation guides
- Research emerging AI technologies and recommend tools and best practices
- Design AI-driven monitoring solutions analyzing logs to predict system issues, automate RCA, and recommend/automate incident resolution

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience applying AI/ML to IT Operations (AIOps)
- Predictive log analysis
- Automated incident remediation
- Automated root cause analysis (RCA)
- Production AI solutions in enterprise environments
