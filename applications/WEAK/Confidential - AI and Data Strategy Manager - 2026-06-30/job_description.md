## Overview
Lead development, implementation, and adoption of AI-enabled solutions that improve operational efficiency, strengthen decision-making, and drive innovation. Blend of AI product development, governance, data strategy, and organizational enablement.

## Responsibilities
- Lead development and execution of applied AI roadmap aligned with business objectives
- Evaluate, configure, and implement AI-powered applications, workflows, and automation solutions
- Develop MVPs and prototypes using AI tools, LLMs, APIs, and modern technologies
- Establish AI governance frameworks, policies, and responsible AI practices
- Ensure compliance with data privacy, security, and regulatory requirements
- Drive adoption through training, documentation, and change management
- Translate business needs into scalable technology solutions
- Manage technology backlogs and AI-related initiatives
- Collaborate on system architecture, integrations, and future-state technology planning
- Support data governance, data quality, and data accessibility efforts
- Partner with stakeholders across departments to identify innovation opportunities
- Evaluate technology investments and manage AI tool budgets

## Requirements
- 5–8+ years in AI enablement, business systems strategy, automation, product management, technology solutions, or related fields
- Experience implementing and optimizing AI-enabled tools and technologies
- Knowledge of LLMs, generative AI tools, prompt engineering, AI productivity platforms
- Understanding of data governance, data quality, privacy, cybersecurity, responsible AI
- Experience with workflow automation tools, APIs, integrations, low-code/no-code platforms
- Technical proficiency with scripting, integrations, or automation technologies
- Experience in professional services, associations, education, or membership-based organizations a plus
