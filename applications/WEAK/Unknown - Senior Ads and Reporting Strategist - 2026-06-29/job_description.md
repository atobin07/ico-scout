# Job Description: Senior Ads & Reporting Strategist (Client-Facing)
**Company:** Unknown (job posting did not name company)
**Location:** Remote (assumed)
**Type:** Full-time
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-06-29

## Overview
Senior Ads & Reporting Strategist responsible for owning performance, strategy, and client communication for paid media campaigns across Google, Meta, Microsoft, and other digital platforms. Client-facing strategic role bridging campaign performance and client understanding.

## Key Responsibilities

### Client Strategy & Communication
- Serve as ads expert on client accounts; join regular client calls alongside Client Relations
- Explain performance, trends, and strategy clearly to clients
- Translate data into business insights and recommendations
- Proactively identify opportunities, risks, and areas for improvement
- Provide data-backed answers when performance shifts or declines

### Campaign Strategy & Performance
- Own performance across portfolio of client accounts
- Develop and guide strategy across Google Ads (Search, PMax, Display, YouTube) and Meta Ads Manager
- Optimize based on performance data, lead quality, and client goals
- Continuously test and refine targeting, messaging, campaign structure
- Identify and reduce wasted spend

### Reporting & Insights
- Analyze campaign data and reporting dashboards
- Partner with reporting/analytics team to improve tracking and attribution
- Focus on: cost per lead, lead quality, conversion rates, downstream outcomes
- Reporting must answer: What is happening? Why? What are we doing about it?
- Proactively surface trends and risks before clients ask

### Internal Collaboration
- Collaborate and oversee internal team members on reporting accuracy
- Work with Ads Managers on execution and optimizations
- Provide direction and feedback to junior team members
- Improve internal processes and reporting clarity

## Requirements
- 4–7+ years managing and optimizing paid media campaigns
- Strong expertise in Google Ads and Meta Ads Manager
- Experience managing multiple client accounts simultaneously
- Strong analytical, communication, and presentation skills
- Ability to translate complex data into actionable insights
- Proactive, self-directed, accountable

## Nice to Have
- Senior living or healthcare marketing experience
- CRM and lead quality tracking familiarity
- Call tracking, attribution tools, conversion tracking
- Landing page optimization and conversion strategy
