# Job Description: AI Solutions Architect (Salesforce / Agentforce / Azure AI)
**Company:** Confidential (Wealth Management Organization)
**Location:** Not specified
**Type:** Contract/Consulting
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-07-27

## Overview
Senior AI Solutions Architect to lead design and implementation of an enterprise AI-enabled client onboarding platform for a wealth management organization. Define overall solution architecture, identify high-value AI use cases, guide implementation of secure, compliant AI capabilities integrated with Salesforce and enterprise platforms.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead architecture and solution design for AI-enabled client onboarding platform
- Evaluate business processes and identify practical, high-value AI use cases
- Design AI workflows with appropriate human oversight
- Architect solutions leveraging Salesforce Financial Services Cloud, Agentforce, Experience Cloud, and Salesforce Flow
- Design and oversee integrations: Salesforce, DocuSign, Dropbox, Schwab OpenView, and related enterprise platforms
- Define AI governance, security, compliance, and auditability for regulated financial services environment
- Design AI agents for document QC, workflow recommendations, stall analysis, meeting preparation, and intelligent content generation
- Partner with business stakeholders to establish KPIs, measure ROI, prioritize implementation phases
- Provide technical leadership: architecture reviews, implementation planning, vendor coordination
- Develop phased implementation roadmaps balancing configuration, integration, and custom development

## Required Qualifications
- 10+ years designing enterprise business solutions and application architectures
- Extensive experience with Azure AI, Azure OpenAI, Microsoft AI services, or comparable LLM platforms
- Strong Salesforce architecture experience: Financial Services Cloud, Experience Cloud, Flow, Agentforce
- AI-enabled workflow automation and intelligent business processes
- Deep understanding of enterprise integrations and API-driven architectures
- Document management, eSignature platforms, and workflow automation experience
- AI governance, security, privacy, and responsible AI practices
- Consulting, communication, and executive presentation skills

## Preferred Qualifications
- Salesforce Architect/Application/System Architect certifications
- Microsoft Azure AI certifications
- Agentforce or Einstein AI implementation experience
- Wealth management, financial services, banking, or regulated industry experience
- DocuSign, Dropbox, Schwab OpenView integration experience
- AI copilots, intelligent agents, or enterprise automation solutions
