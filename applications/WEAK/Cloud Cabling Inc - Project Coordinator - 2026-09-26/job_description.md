## Role Overview

Looking for a proactive, organized Project Coordinator to help manage day-to-day tasks, coordinate projects, and keep work moving. Works closely with Project Managers and Program Managers to turn priorities into clear action plans, track deadlines, organize information, and follow up on outstanding items.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Daily task management:** Maintain a central task list with clear priorities, owners, deadlines, and status updates
- **Project coordination:** Break projects into actionable steps, monitor milestones, and flag delays or dependencies early
- **Scheduling:** Coordinate calendars, meetings, appointments, and reminders
- **Meeting support:** Prepare agendas and materials, capture decisions and action items, follow up on commitments
- **Communication:** Coordinate with team members, clients, and vendors
- **Organization and documentation:** Maintain accessible, up-to-date files, project records, notes, and contact lists
- **Billing and invoicing:** Help prepare invoices, maintain billing records, track invoice and payment status, coordinate with finance teams
- **Progress reporting:** Provide concise daily or weekly updates on completed work, upcoming deadlines, blockers
- **Process improvement:** Create practical checklists, templates, and workflows

## Required Qualifications

- Experience in project coordination, administrative support, operations, or similar role
- Strong organization and time-management skills
- Excellent attention to detail and consistent follow-through
- Clear, professional written and verbal communication
- Comfort using calendars, spreadsheets, shared documents, and task-management tools
- Ability to work independently and exercise sound judgment
- **Prior experience working in the Low Voltage or IT industry (Required)**

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience preparing invoices and maintaining billing records
- Experience supporting Project Managers and Program Managers
- Experience creating task trackers and process documentation
