# Job Description: Salesforce Marketing Cloud Solution Architect
**Company:** LHH Recruitment Solutions (client placement)
**Location:** Remote US (Denver area preferred)
**Type:** Contract (5+ months)
**Pay Rate:** $50–$68/hr DOE
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-07-27

## Overview
Long-term contract SFMC Solution Architect to design, build, optimize, and troubleshoot complex Salesforce Marketing Cloud solutions in production. Hands-on builder-architect role supporting large-scale enterprise digital marketing and CRM transformation initiative.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design and implement end-to-end SFMC solutions: Email Studio, Journey Builder, Automation Studio, Content Builder, Audience Builder
- Architect scalable campaign frameworks for lifecycle, triggered, and customer engagement communications
- Design audience segmentation frameworks, data extensions, relational data models, suppression/exclusion strategies
- Develop advanced personalization using AMPscript, SQL, Server-Side JavaScript (SSJS)
- Build and maintain sophisticated automation workflows; diagnose and resolve production failures
- Collaborate with marketing, CRM, data engineering, and QA teams
- Develop documentation, standards, and governance practices

## Required Qualifications
- 5+ years hands-on SFMC experience (Solution Architect, Lead Developer, or senior technical SFMC role)
- Deep expertise: Email Studio, Journey Builder, Automation Studio, Audience Builder, Content Builder
- Advanced SQL, AMPscript, SSJS, Data Extensions, complex customer journeys
- SFMC data architecture, audience segmentation, personalization capabilities, relational data design
- Large-scale production SFMC environment experience

## Preferred Qualifications
- SFMC + Salesforce CRM / CDP integration experience
- SFMC certifications
- Reusable campaign frameworks, automation templates, scalable architecture patterns
- Enterprise CRM/lifecycle marketing background
