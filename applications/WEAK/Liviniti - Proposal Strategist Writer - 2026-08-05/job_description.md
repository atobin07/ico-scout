# Job Description: Proposal Strategist / Proposal Writer (Remote)
**Company:** Liviniti (PBM industry disruptor)
**Location:** Remote / Work from Home
**Type:** Full-time
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-08-05

## Overview
Proposal Strategist/Writer to manage high-complexity RFx proposal responses for a leading PBM company. Works closely with leadership and SMEs to create customized, strategic, compliant proposals that differentiate Liviniti in the market.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead proposal response process across SME functional areas and senior leadership
- Create new proposal text by interviewing SMEs or using reference documents
- Edit proposal language for compliance and strategic win theme incorporation
- Perform compliance review and quality check of drafts and finals
- Develop proposal calendars and project plans to keep proposals on track
- Provide health updates to leadership; ensure milestones are met
- Schedule and facilitate color team reviews
- Use proposal management tools for stakeholder engagement
- Analyze compliance-driven/technical documentation to determine customer requirements
- Format responses to Liviniti brand standards
- Coordinate proposal submissions by deadlines; develop risk mitigation plans
- HIPAA compliance for Protected Health Information (PHI)
