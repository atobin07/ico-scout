## Job Description

Infinx (healthcare technology company) seeks a Business Intelligence Analyst to collect, analyze, and compile data supporting their revenue cycle management business. Will collaborate with data experts and coordinate data implementation projects with top management.

**Responsibilities:**
- Develop Business Analysis and data recording system for department-wide usage
- Assist team with dashboard and report generation; accurately analyze and collect data for business reports
- Develop performance metrics and examine data reports to locate and resolve mistakes
- Create business reports providing insight into key data points
- Communicate results of data analysis in written and verbal form to managers
- Support marketing, sales, and other departments through analysis
- Monitor data to identify changes in financial and business trends
- Translate stakeholder requirements into reporting deliverables
- Produce MIS reporting and business analytics

**Required Skills and Education:**
- Bachelor's degree in Information Management or related field
- 2–5 years as a financial or business analyst
- Experience in AWS, Quicksight, Microsoft BI, QlikView, Tableau, or other BI applications
- Strong SQL (ability to translate operations needs into optimized query sets), Power Query
- Microsoft Office Suite
- Analytical skills for data-driven report development
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Expertise in data extract/reporting software; metadata, data organization, data quality
