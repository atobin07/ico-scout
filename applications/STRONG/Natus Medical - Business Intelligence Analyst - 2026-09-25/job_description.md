## Job Description

Natus Medical (80+ years in neuro solutions) seeks a Business Intelligence Analyst who can find the story behind the data and turn it into action. Reports to VP of Global Marketing; works across Marketing, Product, Sales, Finance, Service, and Medical Education.

**What You'll Get to Do:**

*Commercial Analytics & Insights:*
- Analyze customer, revenue, pipeline, product, pricing, and market data to identify trends, opportunities, risks
- Structure analyses around ambiguous business questions without predefined requirements
- Go beyond reporting to determine WHY something happened and recommend actions
- Conduct win/loss, customer adoption, pricing, segmentation, and growth analyses
- Model adoption curves and commercial scenarios

*NPI & Market Intelligence:*
- Own NPI performance analysis (revenue, customer adoption, funnel progression, regional performance)
- Build TAM/SAM/SOM models; analyze competitive products, pricing, positioning
- Design and conduct market research

*Business Intelligence & Reporting:*
- Build dashboards and reporting tools for performance understanding
- Define and standardize commercial KPIs, data definitions, sources of truth
- Reconcile information across CRM, Finance, Marketing systems
- Deliver analysis for business reviews, AOP, Product Council, strategic planning

*Communication & Business Partnership:*
- Translate complex datasets into clear, decision-ready insights
- Create executive-ready presentations (what/why/why it matters/recommendations)
- Partner across functions to support business decisions

**What They're Looking For:**
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Economics, Data Analytics, or related
- 2–5 years in business, commercial, marketing, or related analytics
- Demonstrated ability to independently analyze and develop recommendations (not just produce dashboards)
- Strong Excel; Power BI or Tableau experience
- CRM experience (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- Healthcare, medical device, or B2B technology experience preferred

**Benefits:** Health (5 medical options), dental, vision (up to 80% covered), 401(k) 100% match up to 2%, 20 PTO days, 12 floating holidays, unlimited sick, paid parental leave, tuition reimbursement
