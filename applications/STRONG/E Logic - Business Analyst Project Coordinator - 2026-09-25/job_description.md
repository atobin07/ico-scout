## Job Description

E Logic seeks a Business Analyst/Project Coordinator to support IT project delivery within a PMO environment for the United States Capitol Police (USCP).

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Support requirements gathering, user story development, acceptance criteria, and backlog refinement in JIRA and Teams Planner
- Assist with sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, retrospectives, and Agile ceremonies
- Support UAT planning, documentation, and coordination
- Maintain project documentation in Confluence, SharePoint, Teams
- Prepare and update project materials: schedules, agendas, status reports, action logs
- Coordinate project meetings, capture meeting notes, follow up on assigned actions
- Support milestone tracking, timeline updates, and dependency monitoring
- Assist with PMO governance: risk, issue tracking, routing documents
- Provide communication support: draft stakeholder updates and reporting inputs
- Perform general PMO support: data entry, project record maintenance, logistics

**Minimum Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's Degree
- 5+ years administrative, coordination, or project support experience
- Familiarity with project management concepts, workflows, and tools
- IT project support experience preferred
- Strong written and verbal communication
- Microsoft Office proficiency
- SharePoint, Teams, Teams Planner experience
- Ability to organize information and manage multiple priorities

**Preferred:**
- Business Analysis or Agile certification (ECBA, CCBA, CBAP, PMI-PBA, CSM, CSPO)
- JIRA and Confluence experience
- Basic data analysis; process models or workflow diagrams

**Clearance:** Must pass USCP criminal history check, fingerprinting, and background investigation
