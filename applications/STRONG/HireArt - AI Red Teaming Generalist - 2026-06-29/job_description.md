# Job Description: AI Red Teaming Generalist
**Company:** HireArt (on behalf of a global AI development leader)
**Location:** Remote — U.S.-based (excluding California)
**Type:** Entry-level / contract
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-06-29

## Overview
HireArt is helping a global leader in AI development hire AI Red Teaming Generalists to train generative AI models and applications and ensure they provide safe, accurate, and responsible content.

## Responsibilities
- Create original prompts that probe model and application behavior in complex or sensitive scenarios
- Review, rank, and assess model and application outputs for accuracy, safety, and quality across text, image, video, audio, and agentic behaviors
- Identify factual errors, misleading claims, and unsafe outputs; flag and document issues with clear, concise rationales
- Communicate findings with professionalism and audience-appropriate detail; follow writing and format standards
- Collaborate with peers and supervisors to meet project quality bars and deadlines

## Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in a relevant field or equivalent writing-centric experience
- Excellent command of English grammar and style; ability to write clearly, concisely, and professionally
- Ability to work with sensitive or graphic material
- Strong attention to detail; structured, evidence-based judgment using templates, rubrics, and scorecards
- Reliable execution, time management, and responsiveness in a collaborative environment

## Preferred Qualifications
- Writing-intensive fields (copywriting, journalism, editing, education)
- Experience in content moderation, gaming communities, business, and/or digital cultures
- Interest in AI/ML technologies and their societal impact

## Nice to Have

- Secure testing protocols / data export controls familiarity
