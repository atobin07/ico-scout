## Job Description

Navy Federal (FORTUNE 100 Best Companies to Work For 2026) seeks an AI Business Transformation Analyst to ensure enterprise AI investments deliver measurable organizational and member value. The role develops and operationalizes the AI Value Realization Framework and partners with initiative owners to apply it across the AI portfolio.

**Responsibilities:**
- Lead development and operationalization of the AI Value Realization Framework; ensure portfolio initiatives define measurable outcomes aligned to strategic priorities
- Monitor portfolio initiative adherence to the framework; identify gaps, risks, and corrective actions
- Define and maintain value metrics, baselines, targets, calculation methodologies, data sources, ownership, and reporting standards across the AI portfolio
- Develop and maintain dashboards, scorecards, and portfolio reports communicating AI investment performance and realized value to senior leadership
- Develop and review Lean Business Cases, funding requests, cost estimates, forecasts, investment recommendations
- Track portfolio funding, expenditures, commitments, forecasts, and financial risks
- Provide thought leadership on business transformation driven by new technology capability builds
- Liaise between business/journey leads, goal owners, product owners, and development teams
- Solve business problems by interviewing stakeholders, identifying and evaluating alternatives, presenting findings
- Create and present executive summaries of requirements for senior stakeholders
- Influence and negotiate prioritization across business areas
- Stay abreast of FinTech and analytics capability trends; propose innovative solutions

**Qualifications:**
- Experience developing/implementing value realization, benefits realization, performance measurement, or portfolio measurement frameworks
- Advanced skill developing dashboards, portfolio reports, and executive communications conveying AI investment performance
- Experience supporting AI or technology portfolio investment planning, funding governance, business case development
- Experience in banking/financial services industry preferred
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, IT, Computer Science, or related field
- Strong change management skills; ability to influence and set priorities
- Advanced relationship management across business and technology functions
