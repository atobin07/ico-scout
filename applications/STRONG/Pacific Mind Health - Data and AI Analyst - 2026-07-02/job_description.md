# Job Description: Data & AI Analyst
**Company:** Pacific Mind Health
**Location:** Remote / Southern California (outpatient mental health practice)
**Type:** Full-time
**Pay:** Not specified
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-07-02

## Overview
Pacific Mind Health is a fast-growing outpatient mental health practice combining psychiatry, therapy, and advanced interventional treatments. Hiring their first dedicated Data & AI Analyst to consolidate operational, clinical, and financial data into a single source of truth — reporting directly to the Director of Finance & Analytics.

## Responsibilities

### Reporting, KPIs & Scorecards
- Define, build, and own organization-wide KPIs, metrics, and scorecards
- Develop patient-journey reporting across the continuum of care
- Create dashboards, director scorecards, and self-service reporting tools
- Identify and operationalize KPIs aligned to organizational goals and regulatory requirements

### Clinical & Operations Analysis
- Analyze patient access, retention, no-show/cancellation rates, treatment adherence, provider productivity, referral patterns, and scheduling efficiency
- Translate analytical findings into actionable recommendations for clinical and executive leadership
- Support value-based care initiatives through outcomes reporting and ROI analyses
- Build forecasts and early warning indicators for revenue and capacity planning

### Revenue Cycle & Payor Analytics
- Lead revenue-cycle analytics including denial root-cause analysis by payer, location, and service line
- Monitor AR aging and collection-rate integrity
- Track marketing ROI and channel attribution from lead to retained patient

### AI & Automation
- Automate recurring reporting via AI-generated, human-reviewed reports
- Build internal AI tools, agents, and workflow automations connecting core systems
- Pilot and evaluate emerging AI capabilities through measured tests

### Systems & Reporting Administration
- Power user and reporting admin across: Tebra (EHR/practice management), HubSpot (CRM), Asana, Dialpad
- Use Tebra as primary source of truth for clinical, scheduling, and billing data
- Build HubSpot lead/pipeline funnels, Asana portfolio reporting, Dialpad call analytics

### Data Management, Governance & Compliance
- Design and own core data architecture and SQL-based pipelines
- Implement testing, validation, and documentation practices
- Support HIPAA compliance and regulatory reporting requirements

## Required Qualifications
- 2–5 years in data analytics, business intelligence, or healthcare analytics
- Advanced SQL; working proficiency in Python
- Hands-on BI/visualization (Power BI, Tableau, Looker, Metabase, or similar)
- AI-native: Claude Code, Cursor, LLM-powered workflows (APIs, RAG, prompt design)
- Multi-source operational systems reporting (CRM, project management, EHR)
- HIPAA and healthcare data governance understanding

## Preferred Qualifications
- Healthcare/behavioral health/outpatient clinical experience
- Direct Tebra, HubSpot, Asana, Dialpad reporting experience
- ETL/ELT, dbt, BigQuery, or Snowflake experience
- EOS/Traction, OKRs, or Balanced Scorecard familiarity
