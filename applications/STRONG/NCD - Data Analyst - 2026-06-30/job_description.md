# Job Description: Data Analyst
**Company:** NCD (National Congress of Employers / dental & vision insurance)
**Location:** Not specified (remote assumed)
**Type:** Full-time
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-06-30

## Overview
NCD is a top-tier insurance provider (dental/vision) in partnership with MetLife, VSP, and Zurich. Seeking a Data Analyst to join the Data team — turning business questions into trusted data assets, dashboards, reporting workflows, and actionable insights. Stack: BigQuery, dbt, Fivetran, Omni and/or Power BI, Claude and other AI tools.

## Core Values
Relentless Positivity · Growth Obsessed · Get it Done · Helpful Spirit · Solution Driven

## Responsibilities
- Build and maintain dashboards and reports in Omni and/or Power BI for Operations, Sales, Finance, Product, Member Experience, and leadership
- Write and validate SQL in BigQuery
- Support dbt data modeling (build, test, document, maintain models)
- Improve data quality and documentation: define metrics, document sources, perform QA
- Work with Fivetran-fed data; monitor freshness and completeness
- Partner with business teams cross-functionally
- Support operational analytics: billing, eligibility, carrier files, commissions, agent/member data, CRM, payments
- Use Claude and AI tools to accelerate SQL drafting, documentation, QA, and exploration
- Identify process improvements to reduce manual work

## Must-Have
- 1–3 years experience in data analysis, BI, operations analytics, or related field
- Strong SQL: joins, aggregations, CTEs, window functions, data validation
- Stakeholder communication and requirements gathering
- Dashboard/report building and maintenance experience
- Attention to detail and data accuracy commitment
- Clear written and verbal communication

## Preferred
- BigQuery or cloud data warehouse experience
- dbt exposure or interest
- Omni, Power BI, Looker, Tableau experience
- Fivetran or ETL/ELT tool familiarity
- CRM, billing, payment, eligibility, commission, carrier, claims data experience
- Insurance, healthcare, benefits, fintech experience
- Advanced Excel
- Python or scripting (nice to have)
- Claude, ChatGPT, or AI tool experience for analysis, SQL development, documentation
