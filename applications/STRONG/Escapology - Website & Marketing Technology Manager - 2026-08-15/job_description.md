## Job Description

Escapology is seeking a Website & Marketing Technology Manager to serve as the internal owner of our website, digital infrastructure, analytics, and connected marketing technology.

Supporting more than 120 corporate and franchise locations, this person will act as the primary bridge between Escapology's business teams and its external website development agency. The manager will establish priorities, translate business needs into clear requirements, evaluate technical recommendations, oversee projects, test completed work, and hold development partners accountable for quality, timing, cost, and results.

This is not a software developer or DevOps position. The Website & Marketing Technology Manager will not be expected to write production code, configure cloud infrastructure, or personally deploy website releases. However, the manager must possess enough technical knowledge to understand how Escapology's website operates, ask informed questions, challenge agency recommendations when appropriate, and recognize when a proposed solution does not fully address the underlying business or technical problem.

Escapology's current website infrastructure includes a Next.js and React frontend hosted through AWS Amplify and CloudFront, content managed through Strapi, data stored through Amazon RDS and RDS Proxy, code managed through GitHub, and real-time booking functionality provided through the Resova API. The website also connects with numerous analytics, advertising, consent, accessibility, mapping, email, and marketing platforms.
