## Job Description

LBT (defense/tactical equipment manufacturer) seeks a detail-oriented S&OP Analyst to support production planning, inventory management, operational reporting, and cross-functional Sales & Operations Planning. Role bridges Supply Chain, Operations, Sales, Finance, and Design & Engineering.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Maintain the Master Production Schedule based on demand, inventory, capacity, and material availability
- Review sales orders, forecasts, and backlog to set production priorities and customer lead times
- Create and manage manufacturing jobs in ERP system; monitor progress against plan
- Collaborate cross-functionally to resolve material shortages, scheduling constraints, and production risks
- Manage finished goods inventory, safety stock, and slow-moving/excess inventory initiatives
- Lead or support weekly S&OP, planned revenue, and forecasting activities
- Build operational dashboards and reports covering production, inventory, backlog, and KPIs
- Extract and analyze data using ERP systems, Excel, SQL, and other reporting tools
- Maintain ERP planning master data; partner with D&E on configuration management
- Support inventory accuracy, material usage reviews, and continuous improvement

**Minimum Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain, Operations, Business, Analytics, Industrial Engineering, or related (or equivalent experience)
- 2+ years in production planning, scheduling, supply chain planning, inventory management, or operations analysis
- ERP/MRP experience in manufacturing environment
- Intermediate to advanced Excel
- Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills
- Must relocate to / already in Virginia Beach, VA 23452

**Preferred:**
- 3–5 years manufacturing planning or analytics experience
- APICS/ASCM CPIM certification or in progress
- Advanced Excel (PivotTables, XLOOKUP, Power Query)
- SQL, Power BI, or similar BI/reporting tools
- Safety stock analysis, inventory optimization, forecasting, capacity planning
- Defense, aerospace, textile, or regulated manufacturing experience
