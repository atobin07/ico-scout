## Job Description

Stone Industry Insider is looking for a highly capable, technically comfortable person to provide execution support across a variety of projects. The work touches several areas, including media, manufacturing, equipment rental, and software. This is not a traditional administrative assistant position — much of the routine administrative work is already automated or streamlined. What they need is someone who can take projects, loose ends, research assignments, follow-ups, and partially defined problems and help turn them into completed outcomes.

## What You'll Do

- Track projects, open items, deadlines, and things waiting on outside parties
- Follow up with vendors, customers, contractors, and other outside parties
- Research vendors, products, software, consultants, competitors, or business opportunities and summarize findings
- Build and clean spreadsheets, contact lists, and other working documents
- Turn rough notes or ideas into organized action lists
- Help prepare customer or vendor communications
- Maintain project documentation and organize files
- Assist with equipment rental listings, documentation, scheduling, and customer coordination
- Coordinate with video editors and other contractors on media projects
- Support software-related projects by organizing feedback, scheduling demos, tracking deliverables, and following up with vendors
- Handle one-off projects that do not fit neatly into anyone else's job description

Some assignments may begin with: "Here's what I'm trying to accomplish. Figure out what needs to happen and get as far as you can without me."

## What They're Looking For

- U.S.-based
- Excellent written communicator
- Highly organized without needing someone else to organize your work
- Comfortable working independently
- Naturally inclined to close loops and follow up on open items
- Good at researching unfamiliar topics quickly
- Technically proficient and comfortable learning new software
- Comfortable with spreadsheets, cloud documents, AI tools, web applications, and modern business software
- Able to recognize when to make reasonable assumptions vs. when a decision needs to come back

## What This Role Is Not

Not a good fit if you need step-by-step instructions, are uncomfortable learning new technology, or primarily want calendar management or repetitive clerical work.

## Hours & Growth

- Regularly scheduled 15-minute weekly priority alignment meeting
- Room for role and hours to expand if the relationship works well
