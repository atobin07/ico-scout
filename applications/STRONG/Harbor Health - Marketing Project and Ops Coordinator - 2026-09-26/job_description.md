## Position Overview

Harbor Health is seeking a highly organized and proactive fully remote Marketing Project & Operations Coordinator to support the planning, coordination, and execution of marketing initiatives across the organization. This role serves as an operational hub for marketing projects — ensuring work moves efficiently from intake and planning through development, approval, and delivery. The ideal candidate is fluent in using AI tools to improve productivity, communication, and project execution.

## Responsibilities

### Project Coordination & Delivery
- Coordinate marketing projects from intake through completion
- Build and maintain project plans, timelines, task assignments, and status updates
- Track milestones, dependencies, approvals, and outstanding decisions
- Proactively identify scheduling conflicts, risks, and potential delays
- Coordinate project handoffs between Marketing, Creative, Digital, Operations, and other teams

### Marketing Intake & Workflow Management
- Support the Marketing intake process for requests across Harbor Health
- Gather project requirements and ensure requests have information necessary to begin work
- Create and maintain project records within the team's project management platform
- Help improve workflows, templates, documentation, and processes

### AI-Enabled Project Management & Productivity
- Use AI tools as part of day-to-day work to increase efficiency and accelerate execution
- Leverage generative AI for meeting summaries, action items, project documentation, status reporting, requirements gathering, and workflow development
- Identify opportunities to use AI and automation to reduce repetitive administrative work
- Demonstrate sound judgment when using AI-generated information

### Creative & Content Operations
- Coordinate development and review of marketing materials across digital, print, clinic, member, provider, and corporate channels
- Route creative and content for stakeholder, brand, compliance, and other approvals
- Track revisions and consolidate stakeholder feedback

### Reporting & Operational Support
- Maintain accurate project status reporting and provide regular updates to leadership
- Help track project volume, turnaround times, workload, and other operational metrics
- Identify recurring bottlenecks and improvement opportunities

## Required Experience

- 2–4 years in project coordination, project management, marketing operations, creative operations, or account management
- Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
- Demonstrated fluency with generative AI tools
- Experience with project management platforms (Trello, Asana, Monday.com, Smartsheet, Jira, or similar)
- Proficiency with Microsoft Office or Google Workspace
- Experience with creative, marketing, healthcare, or agency teams preferred
