# Job Description: Remote Business Intelligence (BI) Analyst
**Company:** Confidential
**Location:** Remote
**Type:** Full-time
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-07-27

## Overview
Remote BI Analyst to collect, analyze, and interpret business data providing actionable insights for strategic decision-making. Develop reports, dashboards, and visualizations; collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify trends and drive operational efficiency.

## Key Responsibilities
- Collect, organize, and analyze data from multiple internal and external sources
- Develop and maintain interactive dashboards, reports, and visualizations
- Monitor KPIs and identify business trends
- Translate complex data into clear, actionable insights for stakeholders
- Collaborate with business teams to understand reporting and analytics needs
- Perform data validation and quality assurance
- Create recurring and ad hoc reports to support business operations
- Identify opportunities to improve reporting processes and automate workflows
- Present findings and recommendations to management
- Maintain data confidentiality and comply with company security policies

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Data Analytics, Computer Science, Information Systems, Finance, Statistics, or related field (preferred)
- Previous experience in business intelligence, data analysis, reporting, or analytics (preferred)
- Strong analytical and critical-thinking skills
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, SQL, and data visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau, Looker)
- Knowledge of databases and reporting systems
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to work independently in a remote environment
- Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
