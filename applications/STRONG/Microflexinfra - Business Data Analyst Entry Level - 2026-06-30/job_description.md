# Job Description: Business Data Analyst – Entry Level
**Company:** Microflexinfra
**Location:** Not specified (remote assumed)
**Type:** Full-time, Entry-Level
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-06-30

## Overview
Looking for a detail-oriented entry-level Business Data Analyst to support reporting, dashboarding, and business insights. Comfortable working with data, identifying trends, and communicating findings to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Responsibilities
- Collect, clean, and validate business data from multiple sources
- Analyze data to identify trends, patterns, and opportunities for improvement
- Create and maintain regular reports and dashboards
- Use SQL and Excel to extract, organize, and summarize data
- Build visualizations in Power BI or Tableau
- Support business teams with ad hoc analysis and reporting requests
- Document business metrics, definitions, and reporting logic
- Present findings clearly and concisely

## Requirements
- Basic SQL knowledge
- Strong Excel skills
- Understanding of data analysis and reporting
- Basic Power BI or Tableau knowledge
- Good communication and problem-solving skills
- Attention to detail
- Ability to work with business teams and stakeholders

## Preferred
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Analytics, Statistics, CS, Economics, or related field
- Internship, project, or academic data analysis experience
- Basic Python knowledge
- Understanding of KPIs, dashboards, and business reporting
