## Job Description

Valley Fabricators seeks a Senior Business Analyst to drive data-driven decision-making across the organization. The role leverages business intelligence, data analytics tools, and SDLC analysis to translate complex business needs into actionable insights.

**Key Duties:**
- Collaborate with stakeholders to gather/analyze business requirements, translating into technical specs for data models and dashboards
- Design, develop, and maintain reports and interactive dashboards using Tableau, Power BI, Looker
- Conduct detailed data analysis using SQL, R, Python, VBA, and ETL processes (Oracle, MS SQL Server, Talend)
- Perform database design to optimize data storage and query management
- Support full SDLC with Agile project management methodologies
- Analyze complex datasets through linked data analysis and visualization
- Develop comprehensive documentation including process flows (Visio)
- Collaborate across teams on BI initiatives

**Required Skills:**
- Expertise in Tableau, Power BI, Looker for dashboard development
- Strong SQL (MS SQL Server, Oracle); query management and database design
- Advanced data analytics: R, Python, VBA, Bash/Unix, Talend for ETL
- Familiarity with Agile/SDLC methodologies
- Requirements analysis, data model design, data visualization
- Experience with linked data concepts and integrating diverse data sources
- Excel for data analysis and detailed reports
