## Job Summary
Vaco is seeking a Data Analyst for an 18M+ Contract. The role serves as the primary liaison between business stakeholders and project teams, ensuring data accuracy, reporting integrity, and successful business acceptance of project deliverables. Responsibilities include leading data validation and reconciliation, confirming KPI alignment across systems and reports, supporting UAT, coordinating business sign-off, and maintaining audit-ready documentation throughout the project lifecycle.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Data Validation / Reconciliation** — Validating data across source/target systems; performing reconciliation to ensure accuracy, completeness, and consistency
- **Data Quality / Issue Resolution** — Investigating data discrepancies, identifying root causes, coordinating resolution across business and technical teams
- **KPI / Business Metric Validation** — Verifying KPIs, reports, and business metrics against expected results; ensuring data integrity and reporting accuracy
- **User Acceptance Testing (UAT)** — Developing, executing, and documenting UAT test cases, test scenarios, and validation results
- **Business Review / Sign-Off Coordination** — Facilitating stakeholder reviews, feedback sessions, and formal approval processes
- **Audit / Compliance Documentation** — Producing audit-ready documentation, traceability artifacts, and validation records
- **Data Governance / Quality Initiatives** — Supporting data governance programs, quality frameworks, and process improvements
- **Business / Technical Liaison** — Primary bridge between business users and technical teams to gather requirements, clarify issues, and resolve data-related challenges

## Requirements
- Data Analyst / BA / Data Quality Analyst experience supporting enterprise data validation, testing, and reporting
- Data validation and reconciliation across multiple systems/reports
- Data quality management — identifying and resolving discrepancies and root causes
- KPI and reporting validation — aligning business requirements with expected results
- UAT support — test planning, case development, execution, defect tracking, and business acceptance
- Stakeholder review and sign-off coordination across multiple teams
- Data governance, data integrity, and validation best practices
- Documentation and audit readiness — creating validation artifacts and traceability records
- SQL, Excel, or other analytical tools for large dataset analysis
- Cross-functional collaboration with business, project, developer, QA, and data teams
- Data migration / transformation support — structured validation and testing for system implementations
