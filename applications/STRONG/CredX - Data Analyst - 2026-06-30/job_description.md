# Job Description: Data Analyst
**Company:** CredX
**Location:** Not specified (remote assumed)
**Type:** Full-time
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-06-30

## Overview
Seeking a dynamic and detail-oriented Data Analyst to harness the power of data to drive strategic decision-making, optimize business processes, and uncover actionable insights. Transform complex datasets into clear, compelling stories supporting organizational growth and efficiency.

## Responsibilities
- Collect, clean, and analyze large datasets using SQL, R, Python, and other data analysis tools to identify trends, patterns, and anomalies
- Develop interactive dashboards and visualizations with Tableau, Power BI, Looker, and other BI platforms
- Design and implement ETL processes using Talend, VBA, Bash scripts, and Oracle or Microsoft SQL Server
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams in an Agile environment to gather requirements and deliver analytics solutions
- Support database design efforts: efficient schemas and optimal performance
- Conduct business analysis by translating operational needs into technical specifications
- Participate in full SDLC from planning through deployment
- Utilize Linked Data principles to integrate diverse data sources
- Assist in project management activities related to data initiatives

## Requirements
- Proven experience as a Data Analyst or similar role
- Proficiency in SQL (MS SQL Server or Oracle); database design experience a plus
- Hands-on experience with BI tools (Tableau, Power BI, Looker)
- Familiarity with R and Python for data manipulation, statistical analysis, automation
- Knowledge of ETL tools (Talend) or scripting languages (VBA, Bash)
- Understanding of SDLC and Agile project management
- Business analysis experience
- Strong analytical skills; ability to communicate findings to non-technical stakeholders
- Knowledge of database design principles and linked data concepts
