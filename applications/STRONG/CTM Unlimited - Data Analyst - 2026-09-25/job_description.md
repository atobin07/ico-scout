## Job Description

CTM Unlimited seeks a Data Analyst to support dashboard development, data preparation, reporting, and survey analytics for a multi-function enterprise transformation program.

This role sits within a people- and process-transformation environment. Work centers on organizational readiness, adoption, engagement, and program progress metrics — not technical systems performance. Data arrives from multiple functions on different schedules with varying maturity levels.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Build, maintain, and update dashboards supporting program governance, readiness, adoption, and change reporting
- Collect, prepare, clean, and reconcile data from multiple sources
- Maintain recurring reporting and ensure accuracy as new information becomes available
- Track metrics related to organizational readiness, adoption, engagement, and transformation progress
- Support survey design, distribution tracking, data collection, and analysis
- Identify and flag data quality, completeness, or consistency issues to program leadership
- Prepare clear, well-formatted reports, dashboards, and presentation-ready materials
- Support ad hoc data analysis and reporting requests

**Qualifications:**
- 2–4+ years in data analysis, reporting, business intelligence, or related field
- Strong proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets
- Experience with Power BI, Tableau, Looker, or similar dashboard/visualization tool
- Comfortable working with data from multiple sources on different schedules
- AI tools usage (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, etc.) in professional workflow
- Experience with survey data, adoption metrics, or change-related analytics is a plus
- Previous 1099 independent contractor experience is a plus
