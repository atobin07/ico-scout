# Job Description: Data Analyst
**Company:** Celigo
**Location:** Remote
**Type:** Full-time
**Pay:** $75,000 – $115,000/year
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-07-06

## Overview
Celigo is a modern iPaaS platform (Integration and Automation Platform). Seeking a Data Analyst to own the full lifecycle of insight delivery — from translating business questions into analytical frameworks to building dashboards and self-serve reporting for stakeholders. Reports directly to analytics leadership.

## Responsibilities
- Own and develop dashboards, reports, and self-serve analytics (timely, accurate business visibility)
- Conduct deep-dive analyses across revenue, product usage, customer behavior, and operational efficiency
- Partner with Analytics/Data Engineering to define metrics, validate data quality, and ensure reliable foundations
- Establish consistent metric definitions, reporting standards, and documentation practices
- Translate ambiguous business questions into structured analytical frameworks
- Leverage AI-assisted tools to accelerate analysis and automate recurring reporting workflows
- Proactively identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities in the data

## Required Qualifications
- 2–5 years in data analyst or business intelligence role, preferably SaaS
- Advanced SQL proficiency; cloud data warehouse experience (Snowflake preferred)
- BI tools: Sigma, Looker, Tableau, Power BI, or similar
- Familiarity with AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot) to accelerate analysis
- Excellent communication skills for non-technical business stakeholders
- Self-starter in unstructured environments

## Preferred
- Python or R for analysis
- Experience with go-to-market, finance, or product data
- DBT or similar data modeling tools
