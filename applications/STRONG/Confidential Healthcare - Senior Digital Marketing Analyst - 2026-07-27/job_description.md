# Job Description: Senior Digital Marketing Analyst — SEO & Web Analytics
**Company:** Confidential Healthcare Organization
**Location:** Remote (East Coast required)
**Type:** Contract (6 months)
**Pay Rate:** $55–$60/hr DOE
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-07-27

## Overview
Senior Digital Marketing Analyst to support website analytics, campaign reporting, SEO, AI-driven search visibility, and location data management across multiple digital properties. Self-directed professional managing ongoing workflows independently and communicating insights across teams.

## Key Responsibilities
- Develop quarterly reports translating website, campaign, and marketing data into clear performance insights
- Partner with marketing, communications, and content teams to evaluate results across channels
- Build and maintain reporting webpages, email communications, and Tableau dashboards
- Monitor digital performance and identify trends, data issues, successes, and optimization opportunities
- Establish consistent campaign tracking through custom events, UTM parameters, and tagging standards
- Produce recurring data extracts and ad hoc analysis to support campaign and content decisions
- Design and reconcile dashboards illustrating campaign outcomes and the digital patient journey
- Improve organic and AI-driven search visibility through keyword research, ranking analysis, structured data, and SEO platform support
- Maintain accurate online location information by resolving duplicate listings, outdated content, broken links, and naming inconsistencies

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree required
- 5+ years experience in web analytics, digital campaign reporting, SEO, or marketing performance analysis
- 1–2+ years experience with answer engine optimization (AEO), generative engine optimization (GEO), or AI-driven search strategies
- Proficiency with Piwik, Google Analytics, Google Search Console, or similar platforms
- Experience with SEO tools (SEMrush) and location management platforms (Yext)
- Familiarity with Tableau, Looker, Power BI, or another data visualization platform
- Strong knowledge of UTM parameters, event tracking, campaign measurement, and digital reporting workflows
- East Coast location required
