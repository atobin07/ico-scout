# Job Description: Entry-Level Business Data Analyst
**Company:** SoftAppDesigns
**Location:** Remote
**Type:** Full-time, Entry-Level
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-06-30

## Overview
Looking for a driven Entry-Level Business Data Analyst to join a growing analytics team. Work alongside senior analysts to clean, analyze, and interpret data that directly impacts sales, operations, and marketing strategy. Modern tools and AI-assisted analytics are part of the daily workflow.

## Responsibilities
- Collect, clean, and validate daily data from various sources (CRM, ERP, spreadsheets)
- Write and run SQL queries to pull data; build/maintain dashboards in Power BI or Tableau
- Partner with sales, finance, and operations teams to answer business questions with data
- Use AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini) to speed up code writing, data cleaning, and documentation
- Break down complex data into simple, visual summaries for non-technical team members

## Requirements
- SQL: joins, data types, GROUP BY, aggregations (MySQL or PostgreSQL)
- Excel: Advanced (Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, data cleaning)
- BI Tools: Power BI or Tableau dashboard experience
- GenAI tool familiarity for daily workflow acceleration
- Strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, storytelling with data
- Bachelor's degree in Business Analytics, CS, Finance, Economics, or related field
- 0–2 years experience (internships, capstone projects, or GitHub/Kaggle portfolio count)
- Nice to have: Basic Python or R
