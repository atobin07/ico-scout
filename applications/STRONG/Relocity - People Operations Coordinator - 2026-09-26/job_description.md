## About Relocity

Relocity is reimagining the global mobility experience. They enable enterprises to attract, retain, and engage talent globally. Powered by an AI-driven workforce mobility platform, they bring together local experts and insightful content in their native mobile app to deliver an excellent user experience for people on the move. Serves hundreds of cities in more than 40 markets across the US, Europe, and Asia.

## What You'll Do

As People Operations Coordinator, reporting to the HR Manager, you'll provide critical operational support across the employee lifecycle — onboarding and offboarding, benefits, payroll coordination, HR systems and data, compliance, and employee engagement.

- Coordinate onboarding and offboarding across the US, Canada, EMEA, and APAC (document collection, background checks, right-to-work verification, system provisioning)
- Coordinate with payroll team to process employee changes (new hires, promotions, transfers, compensation changes, terminations)
- Maintain accurate and confidential employee personnel files and HRIS records
- Serve as a first point of contact for employee questions related to policies, procedures, leave, and benefits
- Support compliance with applicable employment laws across operating jurisdictions
- Support people reporting and workforce analytics (data compilation, report generation)
- Draft and maintain HR documentation, process guides, and internal communications
- Assist with performance review cycles, engagement surveys, and compliance training
- Assist with employee engagement and recognition initiatives

## Required Qualifications

- Associate's or Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or related field preferred
- 2–4 years of experience in People Operations, HR coordination, or HR administration

- Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
- Ability to handle confidential and sensitive employee information with discretion
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
