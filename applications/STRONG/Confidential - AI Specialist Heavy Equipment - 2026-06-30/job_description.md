# Job Description: AI Specialist
**Company:** Confidential (heavy equipment business)
**Location:** Not specified
**Type:** Full-time
**Posted/Applied:** 2026-06-30

## Overview
Design, implement, and optimize AI solutions to enhance operational efficiency across a heavy equipment business. Focus on workflow automation, service operations, predictive maintenance, and data-driven decision-making.

## Responsibilities
- Analyze business processes (service, parts, operations, admin) to identify AI improvement opportunities
- Design, develop, and implement AI models to optimize workflows and reduce manual tasks
- Build predictive maintenance models to reduce equipment downtime and improve service scheduling
- Develop automation tools for reporting, scheduling, inventory management, and customer communications
- Collaborate with IT and operations to integrate AI solutions into existing systems
- Create dashboards and reporting tools for real-time operational insights
- Train internal teams on AI tools and drive adoption across departments
- Monitor AI system performance and continuously improve models

## Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in CS, Data Science, Engineering, or related field
- 2+ years experience in AI, machine learning, or data analytics
- Experience with operational data, ideally heavy equipment environments
- Experience with AI frameworks
- Familiarity with data visualization tools
- Experience integrating AI into business systems
