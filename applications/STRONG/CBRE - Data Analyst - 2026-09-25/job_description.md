## Job Description

CBRE's Data Science & Analytics team seeks a Data Analyst to perform analysis ensuring recommendations and business conclusions are backed by thorough data research. Part of the Digital & Technology / Data Science & Analytics function.

**What You'll Do:**
- Use SQL and scripting languages to develop/maintain complex queries pulling data from multiple sources
- Develop and maintain data dashboards and associated assets including scripts, tables, workflows, and supporting processes
- Apply fundamental data structures and algorithms to solve complex problems
- Define basic data requirements and gather information using judgment and statistical tests
- Use Python and open-source tools to plan models and extract insights
- Apply modeling and optimization methods to improve business performance
- Develop and maintain data models, data automation systems, low-code solutions, and reporting systems
- Develop ad-hoc reporting from existing data sources
- Confirm quality and integrity of existing data sources
- Collaborate with agile development team

**What You'll Need:**
- Bachelor's degree in CS, Information Systems, or related field; 1–4 years relevant experience
- In-depth experience with SQL, data visualization tools, ETL pipelines, and database management systems
- Strong Python skills for data analysis and evaluation
- Organizational skills with strong inquisitive mindset

**Benefits:** 401(k), Dental, Health, Life, Vision insurance; discretionary bonus

**Note:** CBRE asks that candidates disclose any use of AI in the application and interview process.
