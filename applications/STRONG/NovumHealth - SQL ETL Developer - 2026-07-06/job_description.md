## Overview
NovumHealth partners with Medicaid health plans to manage high-acuity members through behavioral health care coordination. Hiring a SQL ETL Developer to own ETL workflows end-to-end, write production-quality SQL, and partner with analysts and stakeholders to deliver accurate, timely data.

## Key Responsibilities
- Collaborate with leadership to define pipeline specifications; build and maintain SQL-based ETL pipelines
- Write efficient, well-documented SQL queries for extract, transform, and load across systems
- Monthly ETL and database maintenance of cloud-based data tables
- Perform data validation and cleansing
- Troubleshoot and debug data issues in existing workflows
- Partner with data analysts and business users to translate reporting needs into data solutions
- Document ETL processes, data sources, and schemas
- Participate in sprint planning and code reviews

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Business Analytics, Data Analytics, Health Informatics, CS, or related
- Advanced SQL: complex joins, subqueries, window functions, query optimization
- 2–4 years designing, building, and maintaining ETL pipelines
- 2–4 years with at least one cloud data platform (Microsoft Fabric, Snowflake, Databricks, Azure Synapse, or similar)
- Strong communication skills

## Preferred
- Power BI or similar BI tools
- Healthcare data: CPT/ICD codes, HEDIS metrics, NCQA reporting, value-based care
- Python, PHP, Java, or R
- Epic or other EMR/EHR experience
