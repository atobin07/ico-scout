## Job Summary

The Billing Specialist plays a critical role in managing accurate account approvals, maintaining parent-child account relationships, and ensuring data integrity within the billing system. Responsible for verifying account information, preventing duplicate account creation, and supporting timely and precise invoicing processes.

## Essential Job Functions

- Review and approve new customer accounts, ensuring all required documentation is complete and accurate before account activation
- Verify the accuracy of account data captured to maintain data integrity and compliance with company policies
- Update and maintain customer account information as needed
- Generate, review, and process invoices ensuring itemized charges, pricing, discounts, taxes, and payment terms are accurate and compliant
- Scrutinize billing data for completeness, addressing discrepancies proactively
- Work closely with internal departments (sales, customer service, finance) to resolve billing issues
- Respond professionally and promptly to customer inquiries related to billing and account status
- Maintain detailed and organized accounts receivable files and documentation
- Ensure adherence to company policies, financial controls, and regulatory requirements
- Participate in special projects related to billing process improvements or system upgrades

## Required Qualifications

- At least 2 years of professional experience with financial transactions, billing, or accounts receivable in a corporate environment
- High school diploma/GED required (Associate degree in business-related field preferred)
- Proficiency with billing software and ERP systems (Dynamics or similar preferred)
- Strong data entry and data validation skills with high attention to detail
- Ability to analyze account data, identify discrepancies, and resolve billing issues
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills
- Intermediate knowledge of MS Outlook, MS Word, and MS Excel

## Key Attributes

- Exceptional attention to detail
- Ability to prioritize tasks and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment
- Collaborative mindset
- Proactive in identifying potential issues and implementing solutions
