## Job Description

ARCHER Systems (leading technology-enabled legal services company) seeks a Data Entry Analyst to support the Talc Claims Resolution Program (TCRP). Performs high-volume claimant record entry, validation, cleanup, and reconciliation using established procedures.

**Key Responsibilities:**

*Data Entry and Claimant Record Maintenance:*
- Enter and update claimant information in designated TCRP systems, trackers, and workflow tools
- Process claimant updates from approved source documents and authorized work queues
- Apply updates exactly as submitted per documented TCRP procedures
- Maintain clear notes and audit trails for all records processed

*Data Validation and Quality Control:*
- Compare source information to current system records before saving changes
- Confirm required fields are complete and data entered in correct format
- Identify duplicate, missing, conflicting, or potentially inaccurate information
- Complete quality checks and correct errors within authorized scope

*Backlog and Work Queue Processing:*
- Process high-volume TCRP work queues per assigned priorities and turnaround expectations
- Support claimant data cleanup, reconciliation, and backlog remediation
- Track completed, pending, and escalated items using approved logs/reporting tools
- Meet productivity expectations while maintaining accuracy and documentation standards

*Issue Identification and Escalation:*
- Place items in pending/exception status when information is incomplete or cannot be validated
- Escalate complex discrepancies, document questions, system issues to TCRP Operations leadership

**Required:**
- High school diploma or equivalent
- Prior data entry, records processing, claims support, legal operations, or administrative experience
- Demonstrated ability to enter/review high volumes of information accurately
- Strong attention to detail, organization, and follow-through
- Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, Outlook, Teams, and web-based systems
- Ability to maintain confidentiality with sensitive information

**About Archer:** Technology-enabled legal services company providing pre/post-settlement administration for mass tort, class action, and single event cases — including Healthcare Lien Resolution, QSF Administration, claims administration, and medical records review.
