## Job Description

Gallagher seeks an AI Business Systems and Prompt Analyst to translate ambiguous business problems into well-defined AI use cases, build low-code/no-code AI agents and prompts, and operationalize generative AI solutions across GBS functions. Bridge role between business stakeholders and technical AI/ML teams.

**How You'll Make an Impact:**
- Run discovery sessions to identify pain points and decision bottlenecks where AI/automation can add value
- Elicit and document functional/non-functional requirements for AI-enabled systems
- Map current-state processes and define future-state AI-augmented workflows
- Manage AI/automation intake process and maintain prioritized backlog
- Write, version, test, and optimize prompts (system, user, tool-call) across use cases
- Build low-code/no-code AI solutions using Microsoft Power Automate, Power Apps, Copilot Studio, Copilot, and Cowork
- Design prompt frameworks, patterns, and guardrails for reliability and compliance
- Apply responsible AI practices with legal, privacy, security, and risk teams
- Define success metrics, establish baselines, and report realized benefits
- Lead change management: stakeholder demos, training, process documentation

**Required Skills:**
- Experience gathering requirements and translating to AI-enabled workflows (Microsoft ecosystem preferred)
- LLM prompt engineering for document processing, data extraction, summarization, classification, decision support
- Hands-on Microsoft Power Platform: Power Automate, Power Apps, Copilot Studio, GitHub Copilot
- Agile delivery experience
- Python and SQL proficiency
- Strong written/verbal communication

**Education:** Bachelor's degree (Business, Information Systems, CS, Analytics, or equivalent)
**Experience:** 3–6 years senior-level professional
