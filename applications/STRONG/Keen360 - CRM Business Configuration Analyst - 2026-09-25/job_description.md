## Job Description

Keen360 is a software and consulting company specializing in CRM, workflow automation, and industry solutions powered by the Creatio no-code and AI-native platform. They build and implement pre-configured solutions for clients across life sciences, commercial industries, and the public sector.

**Role Overview:**
Seeking an experienced CRM Business Analyst to work directly with clients, translate business needs into solution designs, and carry those designs through configuration, testing, training, and deployment. The role sits at the intersection of business analysis, solution design, no-code configuration, and project delivery.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Engage client stakeholders and understand business processes
- Translate requirements into solution designs captured in a Blueprint document
- Configure lower- to medium-complexity Creatio functionality hands-on
- Lead QA, scope management, client training, and documentation
- Manage delivery from kickoff through go-live

**Requirements:**
- CRM experience required (Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics 365, or equivalent)
- Business analysis / requirements elicitation experience
- No-code/low-code platform configuration experience preferred
- Dashboard and workflow configuration
- Generative AI tools familiarity preferred
- System integrations experience preferred
- Agile methods preferred
- Strong self-management — low-management operating model

**Note:** No prior Creatio experience required; CRM platform experience is mandatory.
