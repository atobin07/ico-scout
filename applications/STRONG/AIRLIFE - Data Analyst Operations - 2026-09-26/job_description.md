## Company Description

At AirLife, they are dedicated to improving the quality of every breath. Premier manufacturer of the highest-quality and market-leading breathing consumables. Expanding global footprint impacting people worldwide. Product portfolio spans the continuum of care from first responder to home care.

## Position Summary

The Data Analyst develops and manages reporting and analytics solutions that support multiple business functions including operations, quality, engineering, and regulatory — ensuring data accuracy, alignment, and compliance across systems. The role balances hands-on delivery of individual analytics projects with a project management focus that coordinates initiatives across the data analytics team. Beyond reporting, the Data Analyst drives process improvement through optimization and automation.
