- 2+ years of experience in website design, content management, or digital design
- High School diploma or GED

**What Sets You Apart:**
- Proven ability to create visually appealing and user-friendly websites
- Experience learning or working with proprietary website-building tools
- Strong eye for design, layout, and user experience
- Ability to quickly adapt to new platforms and technologies
